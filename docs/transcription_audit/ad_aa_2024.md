## Cross-cohort triangulation

Multiple sources confirm the AA-2024 stage progression rates across **five distinct cohorts**:

- **MCSA** (Mayo Clinic Study of Aging, Olmsted County MN) — population
- **ADRC** (Mayo Clinic Alzheimer's Disease Research Center) — clinical
- **ADNI** (Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative) — clinical
- **BioFINDER-2** (Lund University, Sweden) — mixed clinical/population
- **NACC** (National Alzheimer's Coordinating Center) — multi-site clinical

The rates encoded in v1.2.0 represent the best available evidence as of May 2026.

## Methodology change (ERRATA E-2026-002)

Per ERRATA E-2026-002, every ACR in this rule pack was verified against the source paper's methods section to confirm the rate is **annual** (not cumulative). This explicitly prevents recurrence of the cumulative-incidence-as-annual error that affected the v1.1.0 `niaaa_2018` pack (ERRATA E-2026-001, fixed in v1.5.0).

## Notes on copathology

Per Jack 2024 §3, *clinical* staging within a fixed *biological* stage may fluctuate due to copathology (vascular, Lewy body, TDP-43) and reserve modifiers. For audit purposes, the *integrated* stage is treated as monotone. Anti-amyloid treatment effects on biomarker positivity are handled via the separate `ad/aa_2024_trac@1.0.0` rule pack (v1.4.0) using schema v1.2 `required_conditions` mechanism.
