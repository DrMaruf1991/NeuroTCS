## Inadmissible transitions

Per **Jack 2024 §3: "Integrated biological-clinical staging is monotone non-decreasing"**. All backward transitions documented as inadmissible (Stage_2→Stage_1, Stage_3→Stage_2, Stage_4→Stage_3, Stage_5→Stage_4, Stage_6→Stage_5).

## Notes on copathology

Per Jack 2024 §3, *clinical* staging within a fixed *biological* stage may fluctuate due to copathology (vascular, Lewy body, TDP-43) and reserve modifiers. For audit purposes, the *integrated* stage is treated as monotone. Anti-amyloid treatment effects on biomarker positivity will be handled via the `treatment_flags` mechanism in audit v0.2.
