## v2.0.0 changes vs v1.2.0 skeleton

**Breaking change.** The previous v1.2.0 single-axis design (`Stage_0..Stage_6`) is replaced by the Table 7 integrated biological + clinical staging in v2.0.0. The paper's Table 7 is the published vocabulary; v1.2.0 collapsed the two axes into a single ordinal scale, which mis-represented the paper's framework. v2.0.0 encodes the two axes separately and uses the paper's alphanumeric labels verbatim (`Stage_1A`, `Stage_4-6D`, etc.).

The v1.2.0 single-axis design's `transition_priors` (13 verified primary-source ACRs) remain valid for the NIA-AA 2018 pack (`ad/niaaa_2018@1.2.0`), which uses the simpler CN/MCI/AD state space and IS single-axis. The priors do not directly map onto the v2.0.0 multi-axis Table 7 cells, so the v2.0.0 pack's `transition_priors` is intentionally empty. cTCS audit on v2.0.0 is fully functional; pTCS audit defers to the NIA-AA 2018 pack until multi-axis longitudinal priors are transcribed in a future release. See "Historical appendix" at the end of this document for the v1.2.0 prior research.

## Inadmissible transitions (17 total)

### Class 1 — Biological regression without treatment (12 transitions)

For each clinical row (1, 2, 3, 4-6) the three biological regressions D→C, C→B, B→A are inadmissible. Per Jack 2024 §4.3 (verbatim): *"abnormal amyloid PET (A) precedes tau PET (T2), which, in turn, leads to neurodegeneration (N) and clinical symptoms (C), A to T2 to N to C."* Natural history is unidirectional.

TRAC-treated subjects whose tau PET regresses are handled by the companion pack `ad/aa_2024_trac@1.0.0` (schema 1.2.0).

### Class 2 — Clinical regression dementia→MCI (4 transitions)

| From → To | Reason |
|---|---|
| `Stage_4-6A` → `Stage_3A` | Reversion from dementia to MCI not contemplated in Jack 2024 Table 6 staging scheme |
| `Stage_4-6B` → `Stage_3B` | Same |
| `Stage_4-6C` → `Stage_3C` | Same |
| `Stage_4-6D` → `Stage_3D` | Same |

Reversible causes (delirium, drug effect, depression) require human review; the audit flags these for clinician adjudication.

### Class 3 — Genetic-determinism constraint on Stage 0 (1 transition)

| From → To | Reason |
|---|---|
| `Stage_1A` → `Stage_0` | Per §5.2: once Core 1+ established, subject cannot revert to biomarker-negative Stage 0. TRAC subjects whose amyloid PET drops below detection do NOT return to Stage 0 (§9 verbatim: "The underlying AD pathophysiologic process is therefore still active"). |

## External parameters (caller-supplied at audit time)

The rule pack encodes the STRUCTURE of admissibility; three thresholds are caller-supplied per Jack 2024's deliberate non-commitment in §4.6:

1. `tau_pet_mod_vs_high_cutpoint` (required, fail-closed) — moderate vs high neocortical tau PET threshold. Acceptable sources: La Joie 2019 (PMID 30347188), CenTauR Villemagne 2023, Ossenkoppele 2022 (PMID 36357681), local peer-reviewed methodology.
2. `neocortical_meta_roi_definition` (required) — anatomical ROI list per §4.6 ("inferior + lateral temporal + inferior + medial parietal" or Braak-region composite).
3. `amyloid_pet_positivity_threshold` (required, fail-closed) — A+ vs A− cutoff. Sources: Centiloid ≥ 20-25 (Klunk 2015; Jack 2024 §3.1 cites Centiloid ≥ 25 in Mayo cohort); tracer-specific SUVR per FDA package insert; visual read positive; ADAD/DSAD-specific local methodology.

Full documentation in `docs/validation/aa_2024_audit_protocol.md` §2.

## Methodology faithfulness checks (no fabrication)

- ✅ **No invented cutpoints**: the moderate-vs-high tau-PET threshold and the amyloid-positivity threshold are caller-supplied, not encoded in the rule pack. Per §4.6 the paper explicitly declines to commit.
- ✅ **No invented state ordering**: state names match Table 7 verbatim including the `4-6` clinical-stage collapse.
- ✅ **No invented minimum intervals beyond 180d**: the only Δt floor is 180 days for `Stage_1X → Stage_2X`, justified by Table 6 stage 2 "persistent ≥ 6 months." Other transitions have `min_delta_t_days: null`.
- ✅ **No invented inadmissibility**: each inadmissible transition has a `reason` field tying it to a specific paper section (§4.3 unidirectional biological sequence; Table 6 progressive clinical staging; §5.2 Stage 0 genetic determinism).
